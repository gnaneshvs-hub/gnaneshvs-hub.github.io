Case Study

CV-JD Semantic Matching System

Realtek Consulting · Dec 2025 – May 2026

Replaced a manual, one-job-at-a-time recruiter workflow with an automated system matching candidates to jobs at scale — taking accuracy from 60% to 83% across 50,000 documents.

Read the full case study

Problem

Realtek's in-house ATS, Zinnext, serves multiple internal business units with resumes and JDs numbering in the lakhs. Recruiters matched candidates by hand, one JD at a time — slow, and inconsistent across teams.

My role

  • Involved across every milestone: resume ingestion to S3, JD ingestion, moving resumes between buckets as they progressed through stages.
  • Solely owned the AWS infrastructure — EC2 setup, inbound/outbound rules, permissions, and a nohup-based process so the server persists and auto-restarts.
  • Built and integrated a UI into the existing ATS so stakeholders could preview the end-to-end experience before rollout.

Approach

Weekly milestones, then daily ~3-hour testing sessions covering easy, complex, and broken inputs (missing fields, missing keywords, empty JDs/resumes) — tracking failure patterns and iterating on the matching logic with collaborators.

Outcome

Accuracy improved from 60% to 83% across 50,000 documents. The system now auto-generates top-10 candidate matches per job, across multiple open jobs at once — set for full integration into Zinnext.
